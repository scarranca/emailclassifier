A home for your agents’ categories

  • Categories now has its own page. Search your agents’ labels, see downloaded and unread counts, and open a category to browse its emails.
  • Only labels configured in your agents or actually applied by them appear here. Fixed example shortcuts such as Purchases, Work, and Newsletters have been removed.
  • Email rows show your agents’ applied labels instead of built-in category names. Existing Gmail labels remain editable in the reader.
  • Follow-up flags and Jev’s assessments remain separate from Categories.
