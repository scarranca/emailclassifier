An optional cloud copy of recent mail

  • Private pilot accounts can enable Cloud sync in Settings. Your Mac mirrors up to 1,000 downloaded emails from the last 30 days, including labels and Jev results.
  • See sync progress, pause uploads, or remove the cloud copy. Cloud sync starts off and requires your Google sign-in and consent.
  • Drafts, attachments, Spam, Trash and Google refresh tokens stay out of the cloud mirror. Large bodies are shortened.
  • This is the foundation for mobile access. Sync still runs while Cove is open on your Mac; a mobile app and server-side Gmail retrieval are not included yet.
